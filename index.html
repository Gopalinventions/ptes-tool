Design controls

Depth and slopes are design inputs, not geotechnically approved values. The ratio applies to the bottom, not the rim.

Material quantity assumptions

All material inputs are editable examples, not certified specifications. Changing a material label does not change density: enter the supplier's value. No suitability or CO₂ factor is inferred.

Calculated geometry

Rectangular pit with uniform planar side slopes and a horizontal bottom. Water level is rim depth minus freeboard.

Preliminary quantity schedule

QuantityValue

Ideal pit volume is not a surveyed excavation quantity. Terrain, embankments, anchor trenches, drains, penetrations and construction working space are excluded. Liner covers the bottom and full-height side walls. Insulation follows the water-surface footprint. These quantities are not a bill of construction works.

Engineering review required

Before construction: confirm terrain, groundwater uplift, slope stability, settlement, liner temperature compatibility, cover drainage/moisture control and structural details. Carbon calculations will follow verified quantities and product-specific datasets; no emissions are calculated in this version.
